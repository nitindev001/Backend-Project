Why we built Recall

It started as a college project — a MERN app to auto-generate quizzes from lecture notes. But quizzes alone weren't enough; we kept forgetting the same answers weeks later.

So we added spaced repetition: every question comes back exactly when you're about to forget it, not a day too early or too late. That's the whole idea behind Recall.
